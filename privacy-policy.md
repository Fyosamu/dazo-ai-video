# Privacy Policy - Chess YT Bot

Last updated: September 2026

This application accesses your Google account data only to upload videos to your own YouTube channel after you grant explicit consent.

## Data we access
- YouTube video upload permission (youtube.upload)
- Your Google account email address (for OAuth identification)

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell, share, or rent your personal data.
- We do not store your Google credentials on third-party servers.
- We do not access your private videos beyond uploading.

## Data storage
Only an OAuth refresh token is stored locally, used solely to obtain short-lived upload access. You may revoke access at any time from your Google Account security settings.
